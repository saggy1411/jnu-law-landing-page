Frequently Asked Questions

B.A. LL.B. combines law with the humanities and social sciences, including history, political science and economics. B.B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) combines law with business administration, so business principles sit alongside legal education.

For B.A. LL.B. and B.B.A. LL.B. (Hons.), you need 10+2 in any stream or a valid CLAT score. Selection is by entrance test and personal interview.

The LL.B. is a three-year programme for graduates. You need a degree in any stream with at least 45%, and selection is by merit and personal interview.

The LL.M. is a one-year programme for LL.B. graduates with at least 50%. Selection is by entrance test and personal interview. You can specialise in Intellectual Property Law, Criminal and Security Laws, Corporate and Commercial Laws, or Constitutional Law.

Graduates go on to legal practice, judicial services, corporate counsel roles, legal consultancy, government legal advisory, human rights advocacy, legal research and academia.

You will take part in moot courts, mock trials and mediation and arbitration competitions, and visit the District Court, the High Court in Jaipur and the Supreme Court in New Delhi. Moot court is a compulsory subject in the B.A. LL.B., and internships are required.

Scholarships are available as per university norms, including merit-based scholarships. Contact the admissions team to check your eligibility.
